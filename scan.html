Enter your student ID

You only do this once. This device is then linked to your ID, and next time you are checked in automatically.

Attendance terms

  • Your student ID is saved on this device the first time you check in, and the device stays linked to it permanently.
  • One device can only be used with one student ID. Do not use your phone to check in for anyone else.
  • You can check in once per session, only while your doctor's session is active, and only for your own group.
  • Scan only the QR code shown by your doctor for your lecture.
  • If you entered a wrong ID, ask your doctor to reset the link for your device.
  • If your doctor turns on the location check, your phone's location is read once at check-in to confirm you are in the classroom. Only the distance is saved, never your coordinates, and you are not tracked.
  • Your name, ID and check-in time are stored in the attendance record.
